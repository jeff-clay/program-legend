Privacy policy

Program Legend: Football. Effective October 2026.

The short version

We don't collect any data. The game runs entirely on your phone and never sends anything to us or anyone else.

What stays on your phone

  • Your dynasties, settings and name packs are saved only on your device.
  • Deleting the app deletes them. We can't see or recover them.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No ads, ad tracking or analytics.
  • No location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • No internet connection is used to play.

Purchases

If you buy the full game, Apple handles the payment through the App Store. We never see your payment details. The app only checks with Apple whether the unlock is yours.

Crash reports

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports. You can turn this off at any time in Settings, Privacy and Security, Analytics and Improvements.

Children

The game collects nothing from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, we'll update this page and the date above before the change reaches the app.

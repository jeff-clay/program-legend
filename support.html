Common questions

Do I need the internet? No. Everything runs on your phone.

How do I restore my purchase? Open Settings in the game (the gear on Home) and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with.

Where are my saves? On your phone only. The game saves after every Advance and when you leave the app. Deleting the app deletes your saves.

Can I use real team names? The game's schools are fictional. You can rename any team in Team Name Packs on the main menu, and share packs as files.

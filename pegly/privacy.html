Privacy policy

Pegly: Counter & Countdown. Effective December 2026.

The short version

We don't collect any data. Pegly runs entirely on your phone and never sends anything to us or anyone else.

What stays on your phone

  • Your counters, day counters, history and settings are saved only on your device, where the app and its widgets can read them.
  • Deleting the app deletes them. We can't see or recover them. You can export a spreadsheet (CSV) any time from Settings.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No ads, ad tracking or analytics.
  • No location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • No internet connection is used.

Reminders

If you turn on reminders for a day counter, your phone schedules them itself. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Siri and Shortcuts

If you use Siri with Pegly, Apple handles your request under Apple's privacy policy. Pegly only answers with the counter you asked about.

Purchases

If you buy the unlock, Apple handles the payment through the App Store. We never see your payment details. The app only checks with Apple whether the unlock is yours.

Crash reports

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports. You can turn this off in Settings, Privacy and Security, Analytics and Improvements.

Children

Pegly collects nothing from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, we'll update this page and the date above before the change reaches the app.

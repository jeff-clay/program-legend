Email PeglyApp@gmail.com with your iPhone model, iOS version and what happened. Screenshots help.

Common questions

How do I add a widget? Touch and hold your home screen or lock screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and pick Pegly. Touch and hold the widget to choose which counter it shows. Widgets come with the unlock.

How do I use the Action Button or Control Center? On iOS 18, open Control Center, tap +, and add Pegly's +1. For the Action Button, open Settings, Action Button, and choose Controls. Comes with the unlock.

How do I restore my purchase? Open Settings in Pegly and tap Restore Purchase. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with.

I deleted a counter by mistake. Open Settings, Recently Deleted. Counters stay there for 30 days.

Where is my data? On your phone only. It moves with an iPhone backup or transfer. You can export a spreadsheet any time from Settings.

Why is my count a day off? Day counters can count the first day as day 1. Turn that on or off on the day counter's edit screen.
